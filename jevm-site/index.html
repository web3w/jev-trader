The market, in two dimensionsJEVM / 02

What moves.
What the market does.

An AI Trader could be defined by an intersection: an instrument, a market function and a human-set mandate. The same asset can be traded, quoted, lent or risk-managed.

Assets & instruments What can be traded

  • EquitiesOwnership interests in companies.
  • BondsDebt securities with issuer obligations.
  • CurrenciesCash and foreign-exchange markets.
  • CommoditiesPhysical goods and their spot markets.
  • DerivativesFutures and options tied to underlying markets.

Market activities & post-trade What participants do

  • TradingBuy and sell instruments.
  • Market makingQuote both buy and sell prices.
  • LendingLend or borrow assets and capital.
  • Risk controlMeasure and limit exposure.
  • ClearingReconcile trades and obligations where applicable.
  • SettlementComplete the required transfers.

Definitions: IMF financial instruments · CFTC futures · BIS clearing and settlement · Ethereum EVM

How it would work

From market context to accountable action.

A decision is useful only when the system can explain it, respect constraints and follow through. JEVM connects those responsibilities in one loop.

  1. Observe

    Bring market data, positions and contract state into a shared context.

  2. Decide

    Ask Jev bounded questions and compare possible actions.

  3. Act

    Apply human-defined permissions and risk limits before execution.

  4. Verify

    Track orders, fills, settlement and the reasoning behind each step.
